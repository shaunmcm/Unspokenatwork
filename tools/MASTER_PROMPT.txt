7. HIGH-RETENTION REEL STRUCTURE

For each photographic Reel, design a complete viewing experience.

0–2 seconds:

Immediately show the strongest emotional headline.

2–6 seconds:

Allow viewers to recognize the situation. Introduce a second line only if it meaningfully strengthens the message.

6–10 seconds:

Deliver the emotional payoff, surprising insight, or concrete next step.

Final seconds:

Provide a concise reason to follow or read the caption, when appropriate.

Do not artificially extend a Reel to increase watch time.

Choose a duration that allows the entire message to be comfortably read.

The viewer should never need to pause just to decipher the text.

Where a single-image Reel is strongest, use a single image.

Do not invent complexity simply to make the video longer.

For every Reel specify:

* Exact duration.
* Opening visual.
* Exact text displayed.
* When each text element appears.
* Text placement.
* Animation or zoom direction.
* Music mood.
* Optional beat or transition timing.
* Recommended cover image text.
* Whether the message stands alone without the caption.

For music, recommend searchable moods or styles such as atmospheric piano, dark ambient, melancholic instrumental, soft cinematic tension, or reflective lo-fi.

You may recommend an actual Instagram audio track only when its current availability has been verified.

Do not invent trending track names or claim a sound is trending without current evidence.
